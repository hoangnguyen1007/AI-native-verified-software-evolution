# Execute — Work Spanning Routes

Enter after the bootstrap and routing in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Reuse unchanged context.

1. Define the outcome, contracts, non-goals, risk and exit criteria.
2. Use [research](research.md) only for consequential missing evidence and [architect](architect.md) for consequential design. Preserve approvals already given.
3. Use [implement](implement.md) for authorized edits.
4. Use [verify](verify.md) for the final affected state. In implementation mode, repair supported in-scope failures and reverify. A separately assigned verifier reports findings and stays read-only.
5. Use [review](review.md) when risk warrants adversarial review. Self-review is allowed but must not be called independent.
6. Update durable state and produce one [handoff](handoff.md).

Stop at the requested outcome. Do not automatically begin a new milestone or turn review findings into edits. On an external blocker or repeated non-progress, state the evidence, attempted remedies and exact next action.
