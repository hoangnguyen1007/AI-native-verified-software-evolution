Pre-warmed read-only Maven mirrors, lower hot-graph cache eviction thresholds, and transitive BOM resolution tuning remain active scaling work.
2. **Mega-Scale Streaming Horizon (`RQ-6`, e.g., `ThingsBoard ~1.95M LOC`):** Full online Docker streaming benchmarks at `>1M LOC` scale remain a dedicated future evaluation campaign beyond `E8`'s `406.6k`-node `Shopizer` milestone.
3. **Live Upstream HEAD Drift vs Frozen `EMSE2025SAR` Ground Truth:** Because Evo Online Web App clones live upstream repositories, post-freeze upstream changes (e.g., `spring-petclinic-microservices`'s new `genai` service accounting for `1/4` total Batch 11 COMP FPs and `7/34` total CONN FPs) are honestly retained without pinning overrides or cherry-picking.
4. **3-Way Timing Execution Mode:** `E8` Part 2 ran `EVO_ONLINE_MODE=false` inside `container_local_overlayfs` on the exact `evo-workbench-online:latest` image (`5cdb07ad0f6c`) solely because `--online` `GitHostPolicy` intentionally forbids local/RFC1918 Git URLs to prevent SSRF; a future public-remote two-commit fixture can exercise the identical 3-way check under `EVO_ONLINE_MODE=true`.
5. **Working-Tree `cli/` Accounting:** Zero Batch 9–11 edits touched `cli/`; 3 pre-existing uncommitted `cli/` files against `HEAD a661db3` remain documented transparently until the human decides commit boundaries.
